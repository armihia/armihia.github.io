About Me

I’m a PhD researcher at the University of Exeter, supervised by Dr Siyang Song.

I’m working toward an artificial general intelligence (AGI) agent that models human cognition and consciousness and draws on experiences it accumulates in a simulated world.

I plan to connect the evolving internal states studied in Intentional Agent with the way PersMem models which experiences an agent remembers. In sandboxes such as CMASE, I want to test how social experience shapes an agent’s memory, interpretation, and behaviour over time.

I draw on psychology, phenomenology, and sociology when designing these models and experiments.
